# System

Brand book v1.4. This is the source for colours, type, logo and layout. Use these values exactly. Do not colour a client's product with these colours.

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#0B0C0F` | Ground. Backgrounds, text on paper, the Werkplek itself |
| Paper | `#F2EFE6` | Light surface. Print and documents |
| Gold / cut | `#CBA56A` | Signal: one accent per surface, for a button, the cut or focus. Always this colour in the logo |
| Violet | `#6A4CFF` | Second accent in product interfaces only: data, tags. Use sparingly. Never in the logo |
| Steel | `#8A8F98` | Meta, labels, captions, disabled |
| Fog | `#16181E` | Raised ink: cards on a dark surface |

Contrast is ink on paper, or paper on ink. Gold is not body text.

## Typography

| Use | Typeface | Weights | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display and wordmark | Darker Grotesque | Black, ExtraBold, Bold | 40–64 |
| Subhead and intro | Space Grotesk | Bold, Medium, Regular | intro 14–16, heading 20–28 |
| Body text, documents, product UI | Inter | Regular, Medium, SemiBold | 10–12 |
| Labels and kickers (section labels, meta) | Bricolage Grotesque | Medium, Bold | 7–9 |
| Code and hex values | JetBrains Mono | Regular, Medium | 7–9 |

Line height of body text is about 1.45 em. Headings are tighter, about 0.95 em.

If these typefaces are not available: one grotesk for headings and one readable sans for text. No serif, no capitals for the wordmark.

## Logo

- The mark is a C with an angled cut. The ring stands for rounding off and finishing; the gold cut for the decision.
- Primary lockup: mark on the left, wordmark on the right. The cut-C is the letter c: mark + `rietee`. Never `c` + `rietee`.
- The gold cut is always `#CBA56A`. Never grey, never a client's colour.
- Clear space: at least the height of the letter c. Keep that zone empty.
- Minimum sizes: mark 24 px / 8 mm. Horizontal lockup 120 px / 32 mm. Favicon and app icon: the mark only, with the gold cut.
- Variants: primary on ink, primary on paper, mark only, and mono for utility use.
- Never: stretch, rotate, add a drop shadow, or round the cut; redraw the mark; set the wordmark in capitals or another typeface; place the logo on a busy photo without a contrast field; place it in a white box on paper.

## Document layout

- A4, margins 16 mm, 12 columns, gutter 4 mm. One cut may break the grid.
- Background: paper. A cover or heading band may be ink, with paper text.
- One gold signal bar, 6–8 pt, on the left or at the top. Never around the page.
- Kicker: Bricolage Grotesque, with an angled corner of 12° to 18°. The calling skill names the kicker text.
- A card may have one clipped corner, bottom right. Not on every block.

## Other screens

- Crietee's own web pages: maximum content width 1120–1200 px, type scale 1.25. Gold only on the primary button and for focus.
- A client's shop or app: keep their palette. A review document for them still uses this system.
- Business card: front on ink with the wordmark, back with the mark.
