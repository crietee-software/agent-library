# Crietee research

Deliver a decision, not a reading list. Every document follows `crietee-brand`. A memo is in Dutch unless the user asks for English.

Types and source rules: [references/types.md](references/types.md).

## When

Use for: research, research plan, discovery, competitor scan, market scan, spike report, decision memo.

Not for: quote, invoice, code review, a one-off bug fix, fitness week plan.

## Workflow

1. State the decision question in one sentence. No question, no research.
2. Pick one type from `references/types.md` and read that section.
3. Write the scope and the non-goals before you open any source. Default timebox: 4 hours of desk research, or 8 hours for a spike, unless the user says otherwise.
4. Collect sources. Every finding gets a source and a confidence level: high, medium or low.
5. Keep fact, interpretation and recommendation apart.
6. Write the memo as a Crietee Werkplek document, with the kicker `RESEARCH · MEMO`, in the style of `crietee-brand`. The title names the question and ends with a full stop. The first prose is the recommendation in plain words.
7. Check the first and last page of the PDF.
8. Write the location into project memory.

## Required sections

1. Summary: the recommendation in plain words.
2. Decision question.
3. Scope and non-goals.
4. Method and timebox.
5. Sources, with dates. Every figure has a source.
6. Findings: at most 7, each with a confidence level.
7. Recommendation: do, don't, or first X. One choice.
8. Open points that could still change the choice.
9. What this does not answer.

## Do not

- No recommendation without the counterargument.
- No unsourced percentage.
- No quote price in the memo. Point to crietee-quote if the user wants an offer.
